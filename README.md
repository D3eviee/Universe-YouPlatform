**A place for curious minds: stories, books and interactive experiments about how the world works.**

## About

Universe&You is my personal publication about the things that make me curious: science and technology, history, cars, music, health, coding and everything in between.

I started it with one simple idea: learning should feel like exploring, not like studying. So instead of long walls of text, the articles mix writing with images, galleries, equations and interactive experiments you can play with right on the page.

I designed and built the whole thing myself, from the first sketch to the editor I use to write every article.

## What you'll find

- **Stories**: articles that explain big ideas in a way that's easy to follow
- **Books**: reviews and recommendations of books worth your time
- **Interactive labs**: small experiments where you can change the numbers and see what happens
- **A quote of the day**: something to think about, new every day
- **Your own library**: create an account to save articles and come back to them later
